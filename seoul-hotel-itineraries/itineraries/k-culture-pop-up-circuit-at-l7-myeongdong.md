## Day-by-day (3-day sample window)

### Day 1

**HiKR Ground & Gwanghwamun**
- **Morning:** HiKR Ground (K-Style Hub, free): K-pop MV photo stages + VR dance recording.
- **Afternoon:** Gwanghwamun + Cheonggyecheon; K-pop merch stores in Myeongdong (Music Korea, Ktown4u).
- **Evening:** K-pop TV show taping if a free audience slot lines up (check weekly listings).
- **Eat around here:** [Hadongkwan Main Store (하동관 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=192949) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; [Goryeo Samgyetang (고려삼계탕 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=99774) — Classic Korean · Mid-to-Premium.

### Day 2

**HYBE, Yongsan & PC bangs**
- **Morning:** HYBE headquarters + Dragon Hill walk (Yongsan) — mostly exterior/shop visit; check cafe hours.
- **Afternoon:** Yongsan I'Park Mall + e-sports PC bang session (cheap, fun, very Korean).
- **Evening:** Noraebang with song-request lists in English; late tteokbokki + corn dogs.
- **Eat around here:** [Myeongdong Kyoja (명동교자 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=99925) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; [Hong Kong Banjum 0410 (홍콩반점0410)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/홍콩반점0410) — Jajangmyeon · Budget.

### Day 3

**Pop-ups, Mnet & Jamsil**
- **Morning:** Check pop-up listings near Seongsu/Hongdae (rotating; verify current week) — Seongsu is pop-up central.
- **Afternoon:** CJ ENM Center / Mnet studio exterior (Sangam) + DMC Star Plaza.
- **Evening:** Lotte World Tower night view or a K-musical — check nol.world for what's on.
- **Eat around here:** [Goryeo Samgyetang (고려삼계탕 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=99774) — Classic Korean · Mid-to-Premium; [Menten (멘텐)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/멘텐) — Ramen · Budget-to-Mid.
