## Day-by-day (3-day sample window)

### Day 1

**Gwanghwamun museum row**
- **Morning:** National Museum of Korean Contemporary History (free; rooftop panorama of Gwanghwamun & Gyeongbokgung).
- **Afternoon:** Gyeongbokgung grounds + National Folk Museum (free) or National Palace Museum (free).
- **Evening:** Seoul Museum of Craft Art (SeMoCA, free) near Anguk, then Insadong dinner.
- **Eat around here:** [Hadongkwan Main Store (하동관 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=192949) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; [Hong Kong Banjum 0410 (홍콩반점0410)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/홍콩반점0410) — Jajangmyeon · Budget.

### Day 2

**Yongsan & Itaewon**
- **Morning:** National Museum of Korea (free, huge — pick 2–3 halls; English audio guides).
- **Afternoon:** Yongsan area lunch, then Leeum Museum of Art (book in advance) or D Museum.
- **Evening:** Itaewon international dinner + Hannam-dong gallery stroll.
- **Eat around here:** [Myeongdong Kyoja (명동교자 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=99925) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; [Menten (멘텐)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/멘텐) — Ramen · Budget-to-Mid.

### Day 3

**DDP, Euljiro & a show**
- **Morning:** DDP Design Museum + exhibition halls (check current shows); Dongdaemun History Hall (free).
- **Afternoon:** Euljiro indie galleries + K-Auction previews if open.
- **Evening:** Arario Museum in SPACE (Jongno) evening visit, or a musical/performance — check nol.world for listings.
- **Eat around here:** [Hong Kong Banjum 0410 (홍콩반점0410)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/홍콩반점0410) — Jajangmyeon · Budget; [Hadongkwan Main Store (하동관 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=192949) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid.

**Plan B (rain / low energy):** Gallery fatigue: one long lunch at a museum cafe (NMK and Leeum both have great ones) and a river walk to reset.
