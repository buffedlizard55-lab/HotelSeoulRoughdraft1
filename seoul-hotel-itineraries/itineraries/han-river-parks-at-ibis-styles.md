## Day-by-day (3-day sample window)

### Day 1

**Yeouido river day**
- **Morning:** Yeouido Hangang Park: bike along the river (Ttareungyi rentals ~₩3k/hr), riverside cafes.
- **Afternoon:** The Hyundai Seoul for lunch + BANKSY exhibition if still running (through Nov 3), rooftop garden.
- **Evening:** Banpo Hangang Park Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (check seasonal schedule) — picnic dinner on the lawn.
- **Eat around here:** [Hadongkwan Main Store (하동관 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=192949) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; [Goryeo Samgyetang (고려삼계탕 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=99774) — Classic Korean · Mid-to-Premium.

### Day 2

**Ttukseom & Seoul Forest**
- **Morning:** Ttukseom Hangang Park + Seoul Forest Park (deer, butterfly garden; free).
- **Afternoon:** Seongsu-dong lunch and cafes (right across the bridge).
- **Evening:** Eland Han River cruise (Yeouido dock) sunset departure — book ahead.
- **Eat around here:** [Myeongdong Kyoja (명동교자 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=99925) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; [Hong Kong Banjum 0410 (홍콩반점0410)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/홍콩반점0410) — Jajangmyeon · Budget.

### Day 3

**Haneul Park & the sky line**
- **Morning:** Haneul Park silver-grass fields (seasonal peak in Nov) + 291 zigzag sky stairs — arrive early for the light.
- **Afternoon:** World Cup Park picnic or Mangwon Market lunch (nearby, local-feel).
- **Evening:** Han River History Tour riverside walk (runs through Nov 30) or Ichon reed-beds sunset.
- **Eat around here:** [Goryeo Samgyetang (고려삼계탕 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=99774) — Classic Korean · Mid-to-Premium; [Menten (멘텐)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/멘텐) — Ramen · Budget-to-Mid.

**Plan B (rain / low energy):** Cold/rainy: swap to a jjimjilbang day (Aquafield/Dragon Hill) — spa warmth instead of park wind.
