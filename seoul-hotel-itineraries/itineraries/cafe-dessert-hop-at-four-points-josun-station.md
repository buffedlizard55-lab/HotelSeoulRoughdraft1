## Day-by-day (3-day sample window)

### Day 1

**Ikseon-dong & Insadong**
- **Morning:** Hanok-roofed cafes in Ikseon-dong (soufflé pancakes, traditional teas) before the crowds.
- **Afternoon:** Insadong tea houses + ssamziegil craft floors; lunch at a jeon (savory pancake) house.
- **Evening:** Dessert bar hop in Jongno — bingsu/tiramisu modern takes; back to Four Points Seoul Station by Line 3/1.
- **Eat around here:** [Hong Kong Banjum 0410 (홍콩반점0410)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/홍콩반점0410) — Jajangmyeon · Budget; [Menten (멘텐)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/멘텐) — Ramen · Budget-to-Mid.

### Day 2

**Seongsu & Euljiro**
- **Morning:** Seongsu-dong 'Brooklyn of Seoul': warehouse bakeries, specialty roasters (open ~10:00–11:00).
- **Afternoon:** Lunch at a Seongsu brunch/burger spot, then Euljiro printing-alley cafes and nogari beer alleys.
- **Evening:** Euljiro neon at dusk + one more rooftop cafe; DDP Dream in Light on the way back.
- **Eat around here:** [Hadongkwan Main Store (하동관 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=192949) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; [Myeongdong Kyoja (명동교자 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=99925) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid.

### Day 3

**Hongdae & Yeonnam**
- **Morning:** Yeonnam-dong craft bakeries and roastery alleys (slow open — plan 11:00+).
- **Afternoon:** Hongdae style street + free market (Sat), then a themed cafe (animal/cat cafes optional).
- **Evening:** Han River picnic dessert stop (Gyeongui Line Forest Park) then back to Four Points Seoul Station.
- **Eat around here:** [Menten (멘텐)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/멘텐) — Ramen · Budget-to-Mid; [Seoul Local Cafe 1 (서울 로컬 카페 1)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/서울 로컬 카페 1) — Cafe · Budget.

**Plan B (rain / low energy):** Energy dip: skip the third neighborhood and camp at one great cafe with a view — the day still delivers.
