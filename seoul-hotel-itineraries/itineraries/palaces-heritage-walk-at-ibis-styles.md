## Day-by-day (3-day sample window)

### Day 1

**Gyeongbokgung & Seochon**
- **Morning:** Gyeongbokgung (guard changing ceremony; hanbok = free entry), National Palace Museum.
- **Afternoon:** Seochon village lunch + cafes (the quieter west side of the palace).
- **Evening:** Cheong Wa Dae (Blue House) grounds walk if open, or Gwanghwamun Square + King Sejong Story Museum (free).
- **Eat around here:** [Hadongkwan Main Store (하동관 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=192949) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; [Goryeo Samgyetang (고려삼계탕 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=99774) — Classic Korean · Mid-to-Premium.

### Day 2

**Changdeokgung & Jongmyo**
- **Morning:** Changdeokgung + Huwon Secret Garden (reserve the guided slot; autumn peak), Changgyeonggung greenhouse next door.
- **Afternoon:** Jongmyo Shrine (UNESCO, quiet) — guided tour times are limited, check on arrival.
- **Evening:** Unhyeongung + Ikseon-dong hanok dinner; Changgyeonggung evening stroll (check royal.khs.go.kr for any announced night program; the 2026 'Mulbit Yeonhwa' run was in spring).
- **Eat around here:** [Myeongdong Kyoja (명동교자 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=99925) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; [Hong Kong Banjum 0410 (홍콩반점0410)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/홍콩반점0410) — Jajangmyeon · Budget.

### Day 3

**Deoksugung, Namdaemun & the walls**
- **Morning:** Deoksugung Palace + Jeongdong stonewall walkway + Jeongdong Theater traditional performance.
- **Afternoon:** Namdaemun Market (hanbok, crafts, knife-shopping alley) + Sungnyemun gate.
- **Evening:** Namsangol Hanok Village (free, folk games, tea) then Namsan cable car sunset if energy allows.
- **Eat around here:** [Goryeo Samgyetang (고려삼계탕 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=99774) — Classic Korean · Mid-to-Premium; [Menten (멘텐)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/멘텐) — Ramen · Budget-to-Mid.

**Plan B (rain / low energy):** Rainy: palace courtyards swap for museum interiors — National Museum of Korean Contemporary History (free, rooftop panorama) and the craft museum SeMoCA.
