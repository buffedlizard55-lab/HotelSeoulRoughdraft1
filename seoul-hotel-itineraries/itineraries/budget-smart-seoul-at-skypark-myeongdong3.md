## Day-by-day (3-day sample window)

### Day 1

**Free old-city day**
- **Morning:** Gyeongbokgung with rented hanbok (free palace entry), National Folk Museum (free).
- **Afternoon:** Tongin Market dosirak lunchbox (₩5–10k), then Seochon walk.
- **Evening:** Gwanghwamun Square + Cheonggyecheon lights (free); convenience-store dinner stash.
- **Eat around here:** [Hadongkwan Main Store (하동관 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=192949) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; [Goryeo Samgyetang (고려삼계탕 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=99774) — Classic Korean · Mid-to-Premium.

### Day 2

**Market & museum day**
- **Morning:** Gwangjang Market brunch (bindaetteok ~₩5–8k, mayak gimbap), free tastings while you shop.
- **Afternoon:** DDP + Dongdaemun History Hall (free), Euljiro window shopping.
- **Evening:** DDP Dream in Light (free) + street food dinner.
- **Eat around here:** [Myeongdong Kyoja (명동교자 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=99925) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; [Hong Kong Banjum 0410 (홍콩반점0410)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/홍콩반점0410) — Jajangmyeon · Budget.

### Day 3

**River & discount day**
- **Morning:** Han River bike + picnic (convenience-store lunch on the lawn — the Korean way).
- **Afternoon:** Korea Sale FESTA window if running (recent pattern Nov 9–30) — price-compare duty-free vs. Olive Young sale stock.
- **Evening:** AYCE BBQ at a budget chain (~₩20k) or jjimjilbang overnight option (spa + sleep = free hotel night hack).
- **Eat around here:** [Goryeo Samgyetang (고려삼계탕 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=99774) — Classic Korean · Mid-to-Premium; [Menten (멘텐)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/멘텐) — Ramen · Budget-to-Mid.
