## Day-by-day (3-day sample window)

### Day 1

**Suwon Hwaseong**
- **Morning:** ITX/Su-in line to Suwon (~30–45 min), Hwaseong Fortress walls + Hwaseong Haenggung palace.
- **Afternoon:** Suwon galbi lunch (the city's famous dish), then Jidong Market or the fortress cable car back.
- **Evening:** Return to Four Points Seoul Station, late easy dinner in Seoul Station / Yongsan (transport hub).
- **Eat around here:** [Hadongkwan Main Store (하동관 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=192949) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; [Hong Kong Banjum 0410 (홍콩반점0410)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/홍콩반점0410) — Jajangmyeon · Budget.

### Day 2

**DMZ / Imjingak (status-fluid)**
- **Morning:** Tour pickup (book 2–4 weeks ahead; passport required) — Imjingak, the 3rd tunnel if open, Dorasan.
- **Afternoon:** Return by late afternoon; Paju outlet or Heyri art village stop if the tour allows.
- **Evening:** Rest night — convenience-store dinner + movie in the hotel room.
- **Eat around here:** [Myeongdong Kyoja (명동교자 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=99925) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; [Menten (멘텐)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/멘텐) — Ramen · Budget-to-Mid.

### Day 3

**Nami Island & rail bikes**
- **Morning:** ITX-Cheongchun to Gapyeong (~60–80 min); Nami Island ferry + ginkgo avenues (peak early Nov).
- **Afternoon:** Gangchon rail bike (book slot) or Garden of Morning Calm if open.
- **Evening:** Chuncheon dakgalbi (spicy stir-fried chicken) before the train home.
- **Eat around here:** [Hong Kong Banjum 0410 (홍콩반점0410)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/홍콩반점0410) — Jajangmyeon · Budget; [Hadongkwan Main Store (하동관 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=192949) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid.

**Plan B (rain / low energy):** Tour cancellation (DMZ status fluid): pivot to a Seoul fortress walk — Naksan city wall + DDP instead.
