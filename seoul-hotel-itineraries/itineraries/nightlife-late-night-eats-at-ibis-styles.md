## Day-by-day (3-day sample window)

### Day 1

**Euljiro & Jongno after dark**
- **Afternoon:** Late brunch, then Euljiro 'Hipjiro' printing alleys by daylight to scout the bars.
- **Evening:** Nogari beer + grilled pollack alley crawl; hop between Euljiro 3-ga craft bars; noraebang (singing room) session.
- **Night:** 24-hour soup or ramyeon near Ibis Styles Myeongdong — the classic Seoul 2 AM meal.
- **Eat around here:** [Hadongkwan Main Store (하동관 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=192949) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; [Goryeo Samgyetang (고려삼계탕 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=99774) — Classic Korean · Mid-to-Premium.

### Day 2

**Hongdae streets & clubs**
- **Afternoon:** Slow start, Hongdae street food + busking around 18:00–20:00.
- **Evening:** Live music bars (check post-rock/indie listings), then Hongdae club row or a late pojangmacha tent.
- **Night:** Last Line 2 train toward Myeongdong, or 24-hour gopchang/samgyeopsal near the hotel.
- **Eat around here:** [Myeongdong Kyoja (명동교자 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=99925) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; [Hong Kong Banjum 0410 (홍콩반점0410)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/홍콩반점0410) — Jajangmyeon · Budget.

### Day 3

**Shows, DDP & night markets**
- **Afternoon:** Afternoon show: JUMP martial-arts comedy or CHEF (Bibap) — both non-verbal, daily.
- **Evening:** DDP Dream in Light (free, nightly) + Dongdaemun night market.
- **Night:** Late-night fried chicken (chimaek) delivery to the hotel — the true Korean ritual.
- **Eat around here:** [Goryeo Samgyetang (고려삼계탕 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=99774) — Classic Korean · Mid-to-Premium; [Menten (멘텐)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/멘텐) — Ramen · Budget-to-Mid.
