- **Eat around here:** [Hong Kong Banjum 0410 (홍콩반점0410)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/홍콩반점0410) — Jajangmyeon · Budget; [Myeongdong Kyoja (명동교자 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=99925) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid.

### Day 3

**DDP & Euljiro indoors**

- **Evening:** Korean-Japanese dinner (ramen) near Four Points Seoul Station — Menten or a local favorite, then convenience-store dessert.
- **Eat around here:** [Hadongkwan Main Store (하동관 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=192949) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; [Seoul Local Ramen 1 (서울 로컬 라멘 1)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/서울 로컬 라멘 1) — Ramen · Budget.
