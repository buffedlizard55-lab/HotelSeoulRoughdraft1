## Day-by-day (3-day sample window)

### Day 1

**Jongno to Euljiro loop**
- **Morning:** Jongno 3-ga lunch market + tapgol park; wander the printing/paper alleys.
- **Afternoon:** Euljiro craft workshops and indie galleries.
- **Evening:** Euljiro nogari beer alleys + pajeon; night bus/walk back to Seoul Station / Yongsan (transport hub).
- **Eat around here:** [Hadongkwan Main Store (하동관 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=192949) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; [Hong Kong Banjum 0410 (홍콩반점0410)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/홍콩반점0410) — Jajangmyeon · Budget.

### Day 2

**Mapo-Gongdeok loop**
- **Morning:** Mapo meat alley + Gongdeok food street (beyond tourist maps).
- **Afternoon:** Gyeongui Line Forest Park / Yeontral Park walk to Hongdae.
- **Evening:** Hongdae free market (Sat), busking, indie stages.
- **Eat around here:** [Myeongdong Kyoja (명동교자 본점)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=99925) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; [Menten (멘텐)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/멘텐) — Ramen · Budget-to-Mid.

### Day 3

**Seongsu to Seoul Forest**
- **Morning:** Seongsu-dong factories-turned-cafes and flagship pop-ups.
- **Afternoon:** Seoul Forest Park + Under Stand Avenue container shops.
- **Evening:** Sungsu evening restaurants (gopchang, pasta, wine bars) then home.
- **Eat around here:** [Hong Kong Banjum 0410 (홍콩반점0410)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/홍콩반점0410) — Jajangmyeon · Budget; [Seoul Local Classic Korean 1 (서울 로컬 한식 1)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/서울 로컬 한식 1) — Classic Korean · Budget.
