Day-by-day (3-day sample window)

Day 1

Gwangjang & Dongdaemun

  • Morning: Gwangjang Market brunch: bindaetteok, mayak gimbap, yukhoe, mung-bean pancakes.
  • Afternoon: Walk the Cheonggyecheon to Dongdaemun — grilled fish street (saengseon-gui alley) and the toy/stationery wholesale market for snacks and souvenirs.
  • Evening: Jangchung-dong jokbal (glazed pork trotter) alley — a local institution; late tteokbokki near Ibis Styles Myeongdong.
  • Eat around here: Hadongkwan Main Store (하동관 본점) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; Goryeo Samgyetang (고려삼계탕 본점) — Classic Korean · Mid-to-Premium.

Day 2

Insadong, Tongin & Ikseon

  • Morning: Tongin Market Dosirak Cafe — swap ₩5,000–10,000 for brass coins, fill a lunchbox tray (Tue–Sun 11:00–16:00; closed Mon).
  • Afternoon: Insadong tea street + ssamziegil arcade snacks.
  • Evening: Ikseon-dong hanok alleys: craft beer, soufflé pancakes, and small-plate Korean (jeon, japchae).
  • Eat around here: Myeongdong Kyoja (명동교자 본점) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; Hong Kong Banjum 0410 (홍콩반점0410) — Jajangmyeon · Budget.

Day 3

Myeongdong & late-night eats

  • Morning: Slow start: Myeongdong breakfast — gyeran-ppang, hotteok, and a proper coffee.
  • Afternoon: Myeongdong street-food crawl (tornado potato, cheese corn dogs, egg bread) + a Korean-Chinese lunch (jajangmyeon/tangsuyuk) at a local favorite.
  • Evening: AYCE BBQ night (Korean BBQ all-you-can-eat, ~₩20–35k) near Ibis Styles Myeongdong, then pojangmacha-style street snack on the way back.
  • Eat around here: Goryeo Samgyetang (고려삼계탕 본점) — Classic Korean · Mid-to-Premium; Menten (멘텐) — Ramen · Budget-to-Mid.

Plan B (rain / low energy): Rainy: swap market mornings for an indoor food hall (The Hyundai Seoul food court, Starfield COEX) — same grazing, dry feet.
