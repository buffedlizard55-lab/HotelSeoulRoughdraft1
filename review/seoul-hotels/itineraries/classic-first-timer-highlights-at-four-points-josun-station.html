Day-by-day (3-day sample window)

Day 1

Palace core & old city

  • Morning: Gyeongbokgung Palace (Royal Guard Changing Ceremony, usually 10:00/14:00 — verify) with free entry if wearing rented hanbok; National Folk Museum on the grounds if the weather turns.
  • Afternoon: Bukchon Hanok Village viewpoint walk (arrive before 10:00 for calm streets, residents ask for quiet) then lunch in Insadong.
  • Evening: Ikseon-dong hanok alley dinner and dessert; back to Four Points Seoul Station on Line 3/1.
  • Eat around here: Hadongkwan Main Store (하동관 본점) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; Hong Kong Banjum 0410 (홍콩반점0410) — Jajangmyeon · Budget.

Day 2

Markets & the tower

  • Morning: Gwangjang Market brunch — bindaetteok (mung-bean pancake), mayak gimbap, yukhoe.
  • Afternoon: Cheonggyecheon Stream stroll to Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP), then back past City Hall.
  • Evening: Namsan Cable Car + N Seoul Tower at sunset (book the cable car slot in advance); late supper near Four Points Seoul Station.
  • Eat around here: Myeongdong Kyoja (명동교자 본점) — Classic Korean · Budget-to-Mid; Menten (멘텐) — Ramen · Budget-to-Mid.

Day 3

Change of pace — river or shopping

  • Morning: Option A: Han River bike/ferry morning. Option B: Myeongdong shopping + Olive Young first pass.
  • Afternoon: Lunch near Seoul Station / Yongsan (transport hub), then a second neighborhood — Seongsu for cafes and pop-ups or Hongdae for street energy.
  • Evening: DDP Dream in Light free light show (nightly) or a show like JUMP/CHEF (non-verbal, zero Korean needed).
  • Eat around here: Hong Kong Banjum 0410 (홍콩반점0410) — Jajangmyeon · Budget; Seoul Local Classic Korean 1 (서울 로컬 한식 1) — Classic Korean · Budget.

Plan B (rain / low energy): Rainy: swap outdoor palace time for National Palace Museum / National Folk Museum (both free, inside Gyeongbokgung) and the DDP museum.

If you have more nights

  • Changdeokgung Huwon (Secret Garden) — book the guided slot in advance; autumn peak.
  • Jongmyo Shrine + Seochon village tea-house afternoon.
  • Lotte World Tower (Seoul Sky observatory) or COEX Aquarium if you want a big 'wow' day.
  • Free Seoul Walking Tour (reserve via english.visitseoul.net).
