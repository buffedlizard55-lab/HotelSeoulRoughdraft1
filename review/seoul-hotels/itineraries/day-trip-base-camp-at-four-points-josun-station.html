🚇 Getting around together

Station: Connected via Seoul Station Exit 12 passage; roughly 3–10 min depending on platform. Airport: Airport limousine stops near the hotel (verify route + times); AREX is the rail fallback. Last trains ~23:30 — grab a taxi together if you stay out late.

November is crisp — layers + light shell. T-money for two, small cash for markets.
