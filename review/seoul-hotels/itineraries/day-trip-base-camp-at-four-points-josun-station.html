Day 1

Suwon Hwaseong

  • Morning: ITX/Su-in line to Suwon (~30–45 min), Hwaseong Fortress walls + Hwaseong Haenggung palace.
  • Afternoon: Suwon galbi lunch (the city's famous dish), then Jidong Market or the fortress cable car back.
  • Evening: Return to Four Points Seoul Station, late easy dinner in Seoul Station / Yongsan (transport hub).

DMZ / Imjingak (status-fluid)

  • Morning: Tour pickup (book 2–4 weeks ahead; passport required) — Imjingak, the 3rd tunnel if open, Dorasan.

Nami Island & rail bikes

  • Morning: ITX-Cheongchun to Gapyeong (~60–80 min); Nami Island ferry + ginkgo avenues (peak early Nov).
