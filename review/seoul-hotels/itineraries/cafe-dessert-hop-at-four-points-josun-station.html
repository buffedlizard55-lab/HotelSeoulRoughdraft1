🗓 Your 3-day sample together

Day 1Ikseon-dong & Insadong
morning

☀️ Hanok-roofed cafes in Ikseon-dong (soufflé pancakes, traditional teas) before the crowds.

afternoon

🏙️ Insadong tea houses + ssamziegil craft floors; lunch at a jeon (savory pancake) house.

evening

🌇 Dessert bar hop in Jongno — bingsu/tiramisu modern takes; back to Four Points Seoul Station by Line 3/1.

🍜 Together bite: Hong Kong Banjum 0410 and Menten — both a short walk from Four Points Seoul Station.
Day 2Seongsu & Euljiro
morning

☀️ Seongsu-dong 'Brooklyn of Seoul': warehouse bakeries, specialty roasters (open ~10:00–11:00).

afternoon

🏙️ Lunch at a Seongsu brunch/burger spot, then Euljiro printing-alley cafes and nogari beer alleys.

evening

🌇 Euljiro neon at dusk + one more rooftop cafe; DDP Dream in Light on the way back.

🍜 Together bite: Hadongkwan Main Store and Myeongdong Kyoja — both a short walk from Four Points Seoul Station.
Day 3Hongdae & Yeonnam
morning

☀️ Yeonnam-dong craft bakeries and roastery alleys (slow open — plan 11:00+).

afternoon

🏙️ Hongdae style street + free market (Sat), then a themed cafe (animal/cat cafes optional).

evening

🌇 Han River picnic dessert stop (Gyeongui Line Forest Park) then back to Four Points Seoul Station.

🍜 Together bite: Menten and Hong Kong Banjum 0410 — both a short walk from Four Points Seoul Station.
☔ Together Plan B: Energy dip: skip the third neighborhood and camp at one great cafe with a view — the day still delivers.

🚇 Getting around together

Station: Connected via Seoul Station Exit 12 passage; roughly 3–10 min depending on platform. Airport: Airport limousine stops near the hotel (verify route + times); AREX is the rail fallback. Last trains ~23:30 — grab a taxi together if you stay out late.

November is crisp — layers + light shell. T-money for two, small cash for markets.

♡ Verdict for you two

Pick this if for two cafe lovers — slow mornings, beautiful spaces, dessert second-dates in every neighborhood. It keeps evenings simple near Four Points Seoul Station, with one lovely thing to share each half-day.
